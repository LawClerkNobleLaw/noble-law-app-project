What it does

One session, one place to watch it from.

01

Track it in one dashboard

The full lifecycle, from introduced to chaptered or vetoed. Search bill text and the California code sections a bill touches, redline any two versions, and organize the same bills into client bill books.

02

Alerts tuned to the deadline

A daily digest grouped by client, alert rules by keyword that adopt newly matching bills, and the Legislature's deadline calendar laid over your tracked bills so nothing slips heading into a deadline week.

03

Draft the letter from the bill

Support and opposition letters drafted from the bill and your client's recorded position, in your letterhead. You review and send — nothing goes out on its own.

04

Positions on the record

Take a position, attach it to a client, and keep a timestamped history of exactly when it changed and who changed it — the paper trail a filing and a partner both ask for.

An FPPC Form 601 drafted from the registration and activity you already logged, with a human sign-off gate before anything is considered ready. Rotunda prepares — it never files for you.

Questions

The honest version.

Where does the bill data come from?

Official California legislative records by way of LegiScan: bill text and versions, status history, committee actions, and roll-call votes, refreshed as the Legislature posts them.

How often does status update?

Every flagged bill is checked daily. Amendments, referrals, votes, and newly set hearing dates arrive in the digest and on the calendar.

Are the forms filed for me?

No. Rotunda drafts and versions them from your own records. You review, sign off, and file with the Secretary of State. There is no e-file integration, by design.

Can other people on my team see my positions?

On a firm account, client records and positions are shared across everyone at the firm — the same list, notes, and positions. Per-matter isolation between colleagues is a future change, not how it works today.

Does it write the letters for me?

It drafts a first version from the bill and your client's recorded position, in your letterhead. You edit, review, and send. It never sends anything on its own.

Can I bring my own contact spreadsheet?

Yes. Import a crowdsourced-style staff directory and it maps into searchable records with committee, caucus, and issue-area assignments, then exports to your phone's contacts.
